# Consolidated End-to-End Study Summary & Evidence Index (2026-11-11)

## 1. Study Scope & Honest Scientific Boundaries
This document packages the completed end-to-end recorded-EEG-to-robot engineering study across PhysioNet EEG Motor Movement/Imagery (`EEGMMIDB` v1.0.0) and the native `robomimic` low-dimensional `Lift` BC-RNN manipulation policy with the Phase 6 one-way completion latch (`+0.015 m` $z$-offset at 10-step bilateral contact + $\ge 0.08\text{ m}$ cube rise).

- **What is established**:
  1. **Cross-subject transfer pretraining improves single-run target adaptation**: Across `15` target participants (`S001–S015`, adapted strictly on `R04` = `30` windows and evaluated on `R12`), pretraining `EA-EEGNet` on `89` source participants (`S016–S109` excluding 5 anomalous/128 Hz IDs) improves mean `R12` balanced accuracy from **53.78%** (`Scratch EA-EEGNet`, `25` updates) to **63.78%** (`Transfer EA-EEGNet`, `+10.00` percentage points, `11/15` participants improved; [evaluation_summary.md](../results/eegnet_training_2026-11-11/evaluation_summary.md)).
  2. **Matched development-only (`R04 -> R08`) comparison across decoder families**: On the `S001–S010` development cohort (`R04` fit only, `R08` evaluation, zero `R12` access), `Transfer EA-EEGNet` achieves **66.33%** balanced accuracy (with 89-subject pretraining), `Riemannian Tangent-Space + LDA` achieves **62.67%** (highest ROC-AUC `0.687`, zero source pretraining), `CSP + LDA` achieves **61.00%**, and `Scratch EA-EEGNet` increases from **56.00%** (`25` gradient updates) to **60.33%** (`200` gradient updates; [results/decoder_gate_study_2026-11-11/summary.md](../results/decoder_gate_study_2026-11-11/summary.md)).
  3. **Why conservative episode-level `CRC-Hysteresis` (`alpha = 0.10`, `n = 15` episodes) collapses to `always-HOLD`**:
     - With `n = 15` `REST` episodes per recording, the finite-sample penalty `(errors + 1) / (15 + 1) <= 0.10` forces **`errors == 0`** (`1/16 = 0.0625 <= 0.10`, whereas `2/16 = 0.125 > 0.10`), placing `threshold` above the 100th-percentile `REST` score before adding `+0.05` hysteresis margin and `0.50 s` persistence.
     - While **59.3%–64.3%** of post-imagery `R08` `REST` episode maxima occur inside the `<2.0 s` post-imagery transition window (`[rest_onset, rest_onset + 2.0 s)`), calibrating `CRC` strictly on pure `REST` interior maxima still yields **`0/10` active subjects** on `Transfer EA-EEGNet` and `Riemannian + LDA` ([results/decoder_gate_study_2026-11-11/summary.md](../results/decoder_gate_study_2026-11-11/summary.md)).
  4. **20-Second Horizon-Aligned Robot Replay & Control Semantics (`42` Saved Rollouts)**:
     - Aligning EEG decision streams, EDF annotations, and step-by-step robot telemetry on the `20.0 s` (`400`-step @ `20 Hz`) episode horizon shows that every successful condition across `S001`, `S002`, `S003`, and `S006` (`24/36` EEG rollouts retained + `3/3` `always-enable` positive controls retained + `0/3` `always-hold` negative controls retained) required **`42–58` executed policy calls** (`2.10–2.90 s` of active policy execution) before the post-acquisition completion latch took over ([horizon_aligned_telemetry_audit.md](../results/project_audit_2026-11-11/horizon_aligned_telemetry_audit.md)).
     - On the actual `20.0 s` horizon, the earlier `(59, 120]` full-recording command-count threshold is retracted: `S002 confidence` (`59` full-recording events) issued only **7** enable events (`36` requested steps = `1.80 s`, `0/3` retained) before `20.0 s`, whereas `S002 raw` (`150` full-recording events) issued **22** enable events (`110` requested steps = `5.50 s`, `3/3` retained with `[52, 44, 48]` policy calls).
- **What is NOT claimed**:
  - No prospective conformal risk guarantee (`guarantee_claimed: False`).
  - No unopened confirmation cohort remains in `S001–S015` (`S011–S015` `R12` were opened on 2026-11-11; [AMENDMENT_2026-11-11.md](../report/midterm/AMENDMENT_2026-11-11.md)).
  - Post-acquisition retention is maintained by the deterministic one-way controller latch (`mode == "latch"`), not continuous EEG-driven carrying.
