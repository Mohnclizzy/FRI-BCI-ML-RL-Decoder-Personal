# Seed-11 grasp-retention diagnostic

The frozen [one-episode replay](RETENTION_DIAGNOSTIC_PROTOCOL_2026-11-03.md) used a copied, hash-verified `model_347.pt`, same task, seed, and 12 s cap. The initial cube pose, joint positions and first policy action matched the preserved seed-11 run. All 1,440 physics samples had valid contact data, no sample gaps, and a pre-reset terminal state. Repeated run trajectories were close but not bitwise identical: maximum cube-position difference was 0.00252 m and maximum contact difference was 0.0648 N. These are observed differences in this replay, not a determinism guarantee.

At 0.225 s right-only contact began while both finger joints were near 0.040 m open. At 0.317 s the cube had shifted 2 cm in XY while still low. The right sensor peaked at 124.5 N at 0.408 s, with left sensor zero and the finite-difference cube speed about 0.65 m/s. The longest lifted bilateral-contact streak ran from 0.9917 to 1.0583 s (nine 120 Hz samples, 0.075 s nominal), well short of the 0.5 s acquisition threshold. Over that streak the sampled cube speed increased from 0.96 to 1.40 m/s and its position relative to the hand changed by about 3.2 cm. At 1.0667 s right contact fell below threshold while left contact remained; the cube continued moving and both contact readings subsequently vanished. The original seed 11 likewise never acquired; its 0 reported drops means no post-acquisition drop criterion was met, not that the cube remained held.

This is evidence of early asymmetric impact and rapid relative motion, not proof of a single failure mechanism or real-world force. Sensor magnitudes are filtered sampled simulator values, and speed is a one-step finite difference of cube-center positions. Hand and finger body origins are not collider surfaces. The replay's [metrics](../results/retention_diagnostic_2026-11-03/metrics.json) contain event states, reset/action checks and raw-trace paths and SHA256 values; the [30 Hz selected trace](../results/retention_diagnostic_2026-11-03/trace_30hz.csv) is subsampled and cannot reproduce every physics-step event. Full unrounded 120 Hz replay telemetry and the isolated checkpoint are in `outputs/isaac/retention-diagnostic-seed11-20261103T2333Z/`.

The result motivated the separately frozen [half-arm inference screen](HALFARM_PROTOCOL_2026-11-03.md). That screen changes relative arm targets, not an enforced velocity or contact-force limit. EEG and HOLD invariance remain untested.
