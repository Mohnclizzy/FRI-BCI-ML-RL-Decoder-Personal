The upstream task mixes near-grasp starts with table starts; our task sets the
near-grasp probability to zero. The short comparison suggests the table-only
start is too difficult for this initial training setup. It does not isolate
reset distribution as the sole cause: the two runs used different iteration
counts and no repeated seeds. The reported success rates are task training
metrics, not a held-out completion benchmark. The final upstream success rate
is about 0.19%, which is still poor.

Remote outputs are under `/home/ubuntu/FRI-BCI-ML-RL-Decoder/outputs/isaac/`
on the Brev instance. `launch.json`, `exit.json`, `console.log`,
TensorBoard events, resolved `env.yaml`/`agent.yaml`, and checkpoints remain
there. The upstream run has its own `console.log` and RSL-RL logs. These
generated files are not committed to GitHub.
